# 0003. Authentication

Status: accepted (studio default)

- **Supabase Auth:** email and password with email confirmation, plus Google OAuth. If the iOS app offers Google login, it must also offer a privacy-focused option such as Sign in with Apple (App Store guideline 4.8).
- **Account deletion** inside the app is required (app stores, GDPR).
- **Turnstile** on sign-up, and rate limits on every auth endpoint.
- **Email:** auth emails go to the local mail inbox during development, and through Resend as custom SMTP in production, since Supabase's default sender is rate-limited.
