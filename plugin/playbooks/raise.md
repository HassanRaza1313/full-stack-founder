title: Raise money
target: a product with traction
time: a day or two of prep
---
1. `/founder:board "should we raise, and how much?"`. **Stop:** raise or not.
2. `/founder:finance model` and `pulse`: the numbers investors will ask for.
3. `startup-pitch` skill: pitch narratives (10-, 5-, 2- and 1-minute), the investor email, Q&A prep.
4. A 10-12 slide deck from the pitch (with a slide or pptx skill, if installed), plus a data-room checklist.
5. Investor update template for monthly use. **Stop:** you send everything.
