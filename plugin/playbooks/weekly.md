title: Weekly rhythm
target: all products
time: about 15 minutes of your time

1. `/founder:listen all`.
2. `/founder:sales pipeline` where outreach is running: today's follow-ups.
3. `/founder:support triage` for live products.
4. `/founder:market calendar`: next week's posts, as drafts. **Stop:** you post.
5. A CEO brief: what moved, what's waiting on you, and the top move for the week.
