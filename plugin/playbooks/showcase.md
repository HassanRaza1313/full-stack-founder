---
title: Showcase a built product
target: product
time: half a day, plus your own posting
---
1. `/founder:verify` (skip if the last run is under a week old and nothing merged since). **Stop:** fix any P1 first.
2. `/founder:legal check` (a privacy notice for the early-access form at minimum).
3. `/founder:showcase <product>`: case study with the early-access form, videos, screenshots, carousel, posts, and the repo made public. **Stop:** approve each public piece, and approve going public.
4. `/founder:showcase <product> demo on` (optional, within the live-demo limit). **Stop:** pick which demo comes down if the limit is full.
5. `/founder:sales prospects --signal` then `/founder:sales outreach`: put it in front of the people it's for. **Stop:** you send the messages.
6. A week later: `/founder:listen`.
