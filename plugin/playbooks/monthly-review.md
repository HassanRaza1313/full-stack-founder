---
title: Monthly review
target: all products
time: about 30 minutes, mostly unattended
---
1. `/founder:listen all`.
2. `/founder:finance pulse` for each live product; `/founder:sales pipeline` for each one selling.
3. Refresh any stale results the next month depends on.
4. `/founder:board "what should get our time next month?"`, with the portfolio as context. **Stop:** set next month's three priorities.
5. Proposed `LEARNINGS.md` lines. **Stop:** approve them.
6. A CEO brief with the decisions, priorities and the first move of the month.
