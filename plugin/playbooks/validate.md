---
title: Validate an idea
target: product idea
time: 1-3 hours, mostly unattended
---
1. `/founder:discover <idea> --quick`: the quick read. **Stop:** continue, park or reject.
2. `/founder:research customers` and `/founder:research competitors` (skip what the quick read already covers well).
3. Playbook `finance-check` (costs, pricing and offer, unit economics, board).
4. `/founder:sales prospects --signal`: find 10-15 people who have the problem, for 3-5 real conversations. **Stop:** you reach out yourself; log what you hear with `/founder:idea note`.
5. `/founder:discover <idea>`: the full run with the refinement loop, using all of the above. **Stop:** Ready to build, plateau, kill, or override.
