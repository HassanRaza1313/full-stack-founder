---
title: Find the first customers
target: product (showcased or graduated)
time: 1-2 hours, then your own outreach
---
1. `/founder:sales icp`: who to target and how to recognise them, from the brief and what `/founder:listen` has heard.
2. `/founder:sales prospects`: a qualified list of 15-25, saved to the media folder (never git). **Stop:** review the list.
3. `/founder:sales outreach`: a personalised first message and two follow-ups for each, drafts only. **Stop:** you send them.
4. `/founder:sales assets`: a one-pager and answers to the likely objections.
5. Each week: `/founder:sales pipeline` to log replies, set follow-ups and feed what you hear into `/founder:listen`.
