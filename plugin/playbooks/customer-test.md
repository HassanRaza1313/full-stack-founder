---
title: Test with real customers
target: idea or product
time: a few hours of setup, then your conversations over 1-2 weeks
---
1. `/founder:research customers` and `/founder:research segments` (reuse fresh results).
2. `/founder:sales icp`.
3. `/founder:sales prospects --signal`: 10-15 people who have the problem. **Stop:** review the list.
4. Interview kit: startup-design's customer-interview method gives a 20-minute script and a consent line, plus `/founder:sales outreach` invites (drafts). **Stop:** you send them and hold the conversations.
5. Log what you heard: paste notes, and `/founder:sales call summary` turns each into anonymised quotes in `research/voice/`.
6. `/founder:finance pricing`, re-run with the stated willingness to pay.
7. Re-score with the new evidence: `/founder:prove` for the idea (it rewrites the verdict), `/founder:discover` for a feature. **Stop:** continue, pivot, or park.
