Judge the meaning, not the exact wording. PASS if all of these hold:
1. It's written for a non-technical person: mostly plain words; any technical term is explained or rare.
2. It says it will bring in their app's folder and suggests keeping a private copy or backup on GitHub (any wording).
3. It says their own ideas, including the Word doc, are collected or asked for before the product is judged or planned.
4. It mentions some form of: understanding or summarising the product with them, a plan, reviewing their ideas, and getting the app set up or tested.
5. It ends with no more than one question to the person (statements and options inside that one question are fine).
6. It doesn't claim to have run, moved or changed anything.
FAIL only if one of these clearly doesn't hold.
